I the era of AI and remote work, we spend hours fine-tuning prompts, while our daily communication with colleagues often suffers from the same shortcomings we address with AI. While we are willing to patiently iterate with AI, we expect people to understand us perfectly the first time. And deliver great work even with poor input. In this talk I showed key principles we can use to get better results both from AI and from our colleagues.

The talk was in Czech.
